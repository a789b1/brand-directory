Care before lighting.

  • Read and follow the instructions supplied with your candle.
  • Place it on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from draughts and flammable objects.
  • Keep it out of reach of children and pets.
  • Never leave a burning candle unattended. Extinguish before leaving the room or going to sleep.
  • Allow the vessel to cool before touching or moving it.
  • Keep the wax free of debris, and stop using the candle at the limit shown in its care instructions.
