Care & use+

Trim the wick to 5 mm before lighting. Keep the wax pool free of debris. Burn on a heat-resistant surface and never leave unattended. Stop use when 10 mm of wax remains. Let the vessel cool before handling.

Care before lighting

Place the candle on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from draughts and flammable objects. Keep it out of reach of children and pets. Never leave a burning candle unattended. Extinguish before leaving the room or going to sleep, and let the vessel cool before moving it. Follow the care instructions supplied with the candle.
