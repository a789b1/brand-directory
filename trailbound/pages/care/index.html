Product Care

Practical care for TRAILBOUND packs, roll-top gear bags and steel bottles, from cleaning the fabric to drying the bottle and cap.

A little care keeps the useful things ready for tomorrow. Always follow the care instructions supplied with the item.

Packs & gear bags

Empty the bag after use, shake out grit and wipe with a soft cloth. For small marks, use cool water and mild soap. Rinse the cleaned area and air-dry fully away from direct heat before storing.

Zips, seams & buckles

Brush grit away from zips before closing them. Check straps, seams and buckle operation before every trip. Stop using damaged equipment and ask for product-specific advice if a load-bearing part is worn.

Steel bottles

Wash by hand with warm water and mild detergent, then rinse thoroughly. Use a bottle brush for the inside. Leave the bottle and cap apart to dry. Do not put a sealed bottle in a freezer or microwave.

A note on conditions

The roll-top gear bag is an organiser for everyday outings. It has no submersion or waterproof certification and should not be used as the sole protection for electronics or items that must stay dry. Use purpose-rated protection when the conditions require it.
