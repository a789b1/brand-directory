Plan a Day Outside

A good day outside starts with a few useful checks. Adapt the kit to the route, conditions and people going with you. This guide is a starting point, not a substitute for local advice or training.

Know the route and the day.

  • Check route distance, terrain, access restrictions and expected time.
  • Review a current local weather forecast and available daylight.
  • Choose a route within the group's ability and agree a turning-back point.
  • Leave the route and expected return time with someone you trust.

Pack around the conditions.

NavigationAn appropriate map, route information and the means to use them. Plan for limited signal or battery.
LayersClothing and weather protection suited to the conditions, plus an extra layer when needed.
Water & foodEnough water and food for the day and a margin for delays. The amount depends on heat, exertion and the route.
Personal essentialsSuitable footwear, sun protection, a light, basic first-aid supplies and any personal medication or equipment required.

Give the kit a useful place.

Put bulky soft items towards the base, keep the load close to the back and avoid loose straps. Keep water, a layer and navigation accessible. A roll-top bag helps organise smaller items; use properly rated protection for anything that must stay dry.

Leave the trail well.

Follow local rules, remain on permitted paths, respect closures, take all litter home and give animals space. Conditions can change. Turn back when weather, time or the group's needs call for it.
