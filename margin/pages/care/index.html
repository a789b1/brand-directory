Product Care

A little care keeps the useful things ready for tomorrow. Always follow the care instructions supplied with the item.

Felt mats

Brush away loose dust and blot spills promptly with a clean cloth. Spot-clean gently with cool water and a mild soap where needed. Do not soak, wring, bleach or tumble dry. Let the mat air-dry flat before using it.

Oak risers

Dust with a soft, dry cloth. For small marks, use a lightly damp cloth and dry promptly. Keep standing water, high heat and harsh cleaners away from the wood. Natural grain and colour vary between pieces.

Desk trays

Wipe with a soft damp cloth, then dry. Avoid abrasive pads and harsh cleaning products. Lift the tray when moving it and protect delicate desk surfaces from trapped grit.

Before placing equipment

Put the riser on a level, stable desk and keep the equipment centred. Check the equipment base and cable clearance. Ask about the current product load guidance before placing a heavy screen on a riser.
