Our Story

A desk holds more than a screen. It is where ideas begin, lists take shape and small decisions become the work of a day. MARGIN brings a little order to that space with a few useful objects.

Tactile, considered, everyday.

Felt softens the surface. Oak adds warmth and a little lift. A simple tray gives small things somewhere to land. We choose a quiet set of shapes and materials that make sense together.

Start with the way you work.

Measure the desk, notice what you reach for and leave a clear area for thinking. Useful organisation comes from your routine, rather than filling every corner.

Care is part of the routine.

Wipe a surface, lift a loose thread, put the pen back. Small habits help the workspace feel ready for the next day.
