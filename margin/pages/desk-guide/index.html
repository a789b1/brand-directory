The Desk Reset

A useful desk starts with your routine. Give the things you use a clear place, then leave a little space for what comes next.

Measure before you choose.

Write down the desk width and depth, then measure your keyboard, mouse area and screen base. Allow room for cables, ventilation and the movement of your chair. Check every product dimension against your desk before ordering.

Build three useful zones.

The working surfaceA felt mat creates a defined area for the tools in use. Choose a size that leaves room at the edges.
The screen areaA riser creates space beneath the screen. Check the equipment base, cable clearance and current load guidance.
The small thingsA tray gathers pens, clips and other loose objects. Place it within easy reach without crowding the main surface.

Keep the tools you reach for close.

Put frequently used items in an easy-to-reach area. Move occasional tools to storage and keep drinks away from electrical equipment. Make one adjustment at a time and notice what feels useful.

End with a two-minute reset.

  • Put loose items in the tray.
  • Clear cups and paper that you no longer need.
  • Brush dust from felt and wipe appropriate surfaces.
  • Leave one clear area for the next morning.
