Which mat size fits my keyboard and mouse?

The felt desk mat comes in 60 × 30 cm and 80 × 40 cm. Measure the keyboard and the full area you use for mouse movement. Leave room around the desk edge and cable openings.

Will my screen base and keyboard fit the riser?

The oak screen riser has a 60 × 22 cm platform and 49 × 22 × 9 cm open clearance underneath. Measure the base and keyboard separately, including clearance for cables.

How much space does the tray hold?

The desk tray takes up 24 × 12 cm on the desk and has a 21 × 9 cm inner area. Measure your longest item against the inner dimensions.

How do I clean the different materials?

Use the product care guide for separate instructions for felt, matte-oiled oak and mineral composite.
