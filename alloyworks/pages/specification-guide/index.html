Specification guide

A complete enquiry helps define the part, the work required and the information needed for a quotation.

On this page

1. Identify the product2. Share the drawing3. Define material and finish4. Confirm quantity and destination5. Agree the final scope
View the collection

1. Identify the product

Include the ALLOYWORKS model number and the intended application. State whether you need the listed configuration or a modified design.

2. Share the drawing

Provide a dimensioned PDF and, where available, a STEP file. Mark the drawing revision, units, datums, critical tolerances, thread standards and mating interfaces. Use a shared file link in the enquiry.

3. Define material and finish

State the material grade, finish, colour reference and any environmental conditions. For enclosures, identify sealing and ingress requirements. For threaded parts, define sealing method and operating conditions.

4. Confirm quantity and destination

Include the first order quantity, expected repeat demand, delivery country and postcode, packaging requirements and requested delivery date.

5. Agree the final scope

Custom pricing, lead time, inspection and shipping terms are confirmed in the quotation. Final production dimensions and interfaces follow the agreed drawing.
