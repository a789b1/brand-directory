Bent and fabricated brackets

Sheet cutting, forming, holes and slots support the L-shaped, U-channel and adjustable bracket families. Specify bend positions, mating faces, hole centres and any weld requirements.

Metal enclosures

Extruded bodies and formed sheet metal provide different enclosure formats. Define panel openings, cable entries, mounting points and access requirements around the equipment inside.

Precision components

Turning, milling, drilling and threading create flanges, bushings and interface adapters. Identify datums, fits, surface requirements and the dimensions that control assembly.

Surface finish and documentation

Specify brushed, anodised, zinc-plated or powder-coated surfaces as appropriate to the material. Request material records, dimensional reports or other documents in the quotation brief so availability can be confirmed.
