Material & handling guide

Handle metal components in a way that protects mating surfaces, threads and the specified finish.

On this page

Stainless steelAluminiumPlated and coated steelMachined interfaces
View the collection

Stainless steel

Use non-abrasive cleaning materials compatible with the finish. Avoid contact with carbon steel debris and aggressive cleaning chemicals. Choose the grade around the intended environment.

Aluminium

Protect anodised surfaces from abrasive contact during handling and assembly. Confirm cleaning and chemical compatibility for the intended use.

Plated and coated steel

Avoid impacts that damage the protective surface. Inspect contact areas and fastener interfaces during installation.

Machined interfaces

Keep bores, threads and mating faces free of debris. Use compatible fasteners and confirm fits, lubrication and assembly requirements with the equipment specification.
