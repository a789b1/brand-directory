About ALLOYWORKS

Metal components should be straightforward to specify. ALLOYWORKS brings brackets, enclosures and precision components into a clear product catalogue for equipment builders and sourcing teams.

A product-led starting point

Compare nine configurations across three series. Each product page identifies the model, material, surface finish and key dimensions, helping you prepare an enquiry around a recognisable component.

Defined around the application

Different equipment requires different interfaces. Use a catalogue model as the starting point, then identify revised dimensions, mounting holes, material grades and surface finishes in your drawing.

Clear requirements before production

Custom quotations depend on the agreed specification, quantity and drawing revision. Critical tolerances, operating conditions, inspection documents and packaging requirements should be confirmed during quotation.
