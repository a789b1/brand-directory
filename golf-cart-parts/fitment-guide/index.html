Identify your vehicle

Club Car

Use the model prefix on the serial number decal to identify your cart. Confirm the model year against the vehicle record, especially for a 2008 Precedent dash.

Club Car identification guide

E-Z-GO

Use the serial number and manufacturer code to identify the cart. TXT and RXV are separate families. For a TXT floor mat, also check the fuel type and horn location.

E-Z-GO identification guide

Yamaha

Enter the primary ID (PID), or select the model and year in Yamaha’s parts finder. Drive and Drive2 use different model names. Check the rear seat and basket layout.

Yamaha identification guide

Check the part before you order

  1. Match the manufacturer part number if you have the original packaging. The catalog also accepts numbers without hyphens or spaces.
  2. Confirm the model, year and configuration in the fitment table. Catalog year ranges cover the vehicles documented here.
  3. Compare the original mount, cutouts and included hardware with the supplier listing. For a converted cart or midyear model, confirm fit directly with the supplier.
