Installation notes

  1. Verify the 54-inch blow-molded roof and check that the bag bracket is in place.
  2. Attach the canopy frame to the bag bracket using the supplied hardware. The mounting arrangement does not require drilling.
  3. Open the cover over the loaded bag area, check zipper access, then fold it away to check that the frame moves freely.
