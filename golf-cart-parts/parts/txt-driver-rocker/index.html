Installation notes

  1. Match the original left-hand rocker shape and OEM number.
  2. With the cart parked, remove the worn driver-side molding as directed by the TXT body service instructions.
  3. Line up the replacement with the original mounting positions and secure it using the fasteners specified for the body.
