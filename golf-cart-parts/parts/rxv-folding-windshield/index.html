Installation notes

  1. Look for the raised driver-side dash contour and compare the frame width with the windshield dimensions.
  2. Keep the protective film on while you test the fit. Attach the supplied mounting hardware to the existing frame as shown in the windshield instructions.
  3. Raise and lower the upper pane while parked. Check that it seats securely in both positions before peeling off the protective film.
Part details

Specifications

Two clear acrylic panels attach to the cart’s existing windshield frame. At 37¾ inches wide, the upper and lower panels share the same maximum width.
