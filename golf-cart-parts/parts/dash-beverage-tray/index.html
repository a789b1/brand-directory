Installation notes

  1. Park the cart and hold the tray between the front struts with the windshield in its normal position.
  2. Mark the bracket locations only after checking clearance behind the mounting surfaces. Use the supplied hardware and kit directions.
  3. Open the windshield and lockbox and check control access from the driver’s seat before loading the tray.
