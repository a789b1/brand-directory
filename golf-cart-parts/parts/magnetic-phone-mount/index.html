Test the chosen mounting area with a magnet. The base needs a flat, magnet-attracting steel surface. Aluminum, plastic and curved tubes may not hold the base. Check the phone and case together against the size and weight limits.

Flat magnetic steel frame or dash surface

Installation notes

  1. With the cart parked, verify that the base grips the chosen steel surface and that the phone clears the driver’s view and controls.
  2. Use a compatible magnetic phone case or fit the supplied ring according to the seller’s directions.
  3. Attach the phone, set the angle and check retention by hand before travel. Make screen adjustments while parked.
Part details

Specifications

The ball head rotates so the screen can be viewed in portrait or landscape. A supplied metal ring adapts compatible phones or cases that lack a built-in magnetic ring.
