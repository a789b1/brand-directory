Installation notes

  1. Compare the width and length of your original webbing and inspect the rack and buckle.
  2. Note the routing of the existing strap before removing it. Thread the replacement through the original rack and buckle in the same direction.
  3. Pull the webbing flat and confirm the buckle holds the golf bag securely before driving.
