Installation notes

  1. Leave the protective film on and compare the screen width and height with the front frame.
  2. Dry-fit both panels on the uprights, then attach the supplied hardware and rubber bumpers according to the kit sheet.
  3. Check that the upper panel stays in place both raised and folded before removing the film.
