Installation notes

  1. Check all four rim diameters and inspect the retaining edge before removing the existing covers.
  2. Align the cover so the tire valve remains accessible. Seat the retainer evenly around the matching rim.
  3. Check that each cover sits flush and cannot be pulled loose by hand before driving.
