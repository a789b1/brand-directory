Installation notes

  1. Park and switch off the cart. Remove loose mats and clean the floor so the liner can seat flat.
  2. Follow WeatherTech’s floor-mounted-pedal installation instructions to position W603 around the controls and seat base.
  3. Check full pedal travel and return before driving. Keep the side drainage vents clear and remove the liner for deeper cleaning.
