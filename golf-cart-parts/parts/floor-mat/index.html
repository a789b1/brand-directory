Installation notes

  1. Park the cart and remove the key. Compare the replacement pattern to the original mat, including the steering, horn and pedal openings.
  2. Remove the old mat following the vehicle manual and seat the new mat flat in the clean footwell. Do not stack mats.
  3. With the cart parked, check full pedal travel and return and confirm that the mat does not interfere with any control.
Part details
