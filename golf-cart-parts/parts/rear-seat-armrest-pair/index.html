Requires a rear seat with square rails measuring 1 in. or 1¼ in. across the flat face. Round tubes do not accept this clamp. Check passenger and seat-folding clearance on converted carts.

Left and right rear-seat handrails

Installation notes

  1. Measure the rail across two parallel flat faces and confirm that both rear handrails have a straight mounting area.
  2. Position the left and right armrests with the cupholders accessible and tighten their clamps according to the kit instructions.
  3. Open the storage lids and check that the armrests cannot twist and that the rear seat still moves freely.
Part details

Specifications

The molded arms clamp to the square side rails of a rear-facing seat kit. The removable cup inserts lift out for cleaning, and the storage lids flip up beside the passenger.
