Installation notes

  1. Count the mounting screws and compare your wheel to the listing before choosing the holder.
  2. Check that the plate can sit flat without blocking the horn or contact with the wheel rim. Follow the hardware directions supplied with the holder.
  3. With the cart parked, secure a scorecard under the clip and check steering and horn clearance.

Specifications

The 6½-by-6-inch writing surface holds a paper card where the driver can reach it between holes. Match the wheel mounting pattern and check the space around its spokes.
