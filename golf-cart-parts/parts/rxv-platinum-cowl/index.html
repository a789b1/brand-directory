Installation notes

  1. Confirm that your cart needs an uncut nose. Compare the existing panel shape and mounting layout, particularly if lights have been added.
  2. Remove the old front shell following the RXV body service procedure. Retain serviceable fasteners and source any missing hardware or nameplate separately.
  3. Test-fit the new cowl against the bumper and side edges, then secure it using the model-specific body instructions.
