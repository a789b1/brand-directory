Installation notes

  1. Park the cart, switch it off and allow hot components to cool before covering.
  2. Pull the cover over the top and ease the elastic hem around the body without stretching it across protruding accessories.
  3. Remove the cover fully before driving. Allow it to dry before packing it into the storage bag.
